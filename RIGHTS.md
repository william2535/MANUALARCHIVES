# Document rights

This public repository contains an independently organised catalogue and links to technical documents. Manufacturer, installer and distributor source provenance is retained. It does not grant a licence to any third-party manual, trademark, software or drawing.

Manufacturer PDFs are not mirrored here. Public availability of a download does not itself establish permission to republish it. Keep downloaded copies in the gitignored `downloads/` directory for use permitted by the publisher. The downloader follows ordinary public links; it does not log in or bypass access controls.

Before adding a PDF to the public archive, record the publisher's redistribution permission or applicable licence, its source, the exact document issue, and a SHA-256 checksum. Retain all notices. Do not apply a repository software licence to a manufacturer's documents.

Do not add paid standards, customer drawings, passwords, access codes, private job records or gated files obtained without authorisation.
