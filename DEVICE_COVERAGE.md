Every addition has a complete-file SHA-256 checksum, measured byte count and PDF page count. Printed title/model information was checked using text or rendered pages. This does not certify the latest edition or suitability for a particular installation. Manufacturer sources are preferred; distributor-hosted original documents are labelled.

Use the category links above, then brand and model. The offline `index.html` search and app manifest contain the same additions. For example, search `KX15DT`, `TriTech`, `6000PLUS`, `ML600` or a Tynetec product code.

## Remaining work

Aico alarm families, further Pyronix DT/PIR manuals, RGL exit devices and additional camera editions are collected for the next review batch. Orisec and some Risco sources have retrieval barriers. Coverage targets common UK trade and legacy service families; this is not an exhaustive inventory or an evidence-based sales ranking.
