# FACP Manuals — complete source collection

[Archive](README.md) · [Search catalogue](index.html) · [Source website](https://facpmanuals.com/brands)

Checked **2026-10-21**: **8,519 source listings across 104 brands**, mapped to **8,519 catalogue records**. **8,519 source PDFs have complete-file checks**. No listing, document type, language or non-fire source was excluded.

The import read 428 brand pages and 17 supplemental title-search pages. Equal-title pagination repeated some entries and omitted siblings; every brand was reconciled by unique source ID against its published total.

## Why the counts were different

The earlier archive had 3,555 total records, including 971 under fire. Its FACP import contained only the 11 Cornell/Jeron documents from the care/rescue pass. FACP lists manuals, datasheets, catalogue pages, brochures and older editions across many additional US and legacy brands. This expansion imports its entire public brand catalogue.

Counts are source listings and catalogue references, not a claim that every item is an installation manual or a distinct technical publication. Exact byte matches may share a catalogue entry; every FACP UUID, title, detail page and PDF URL is retained in the audit. Existing catalogue IDs and model associations are preserved.

## Browse or download

Select **Collection → FACP Manuals** in `index.html`. The category filter includes fire, security, access control, CCTV, care-call and building-controls/other references. Categories are navigation aids; check the original document for applicability.

```bash
python3 scripts/download.py --collection facp --list
python3 scripts/download.py --collection facp
python3 scripts/download.py --collection facp --category fire --brand Notifier
```

The whole collection occupies many GB. Download selected brands if your phone has limited space. The public Git repository contains the catalogue, provenance, checksums and source download links; it does not mirror PDF binaries. Private ZIP volumes can be prepared from the verified download cache using `scripts/package_facp.py`.

## Evidence and limits

[Every source listing and its mapped record](data/source-audits/facp-full-2026-10-21.json) · [Inventory/sitemap reconciliation](data/source-audits/facp-inventory-reconciliation-2026-10-21.json)

Complete-file checks mean the PDF was downloaded, hashed, checked for its header/EOF and parsed for page count. They do not establish the latest issue, UK applicability, verified language, firmware compatibility or redistribution rights. FACP source-listed years remain labelled as such. Its referenced-model lists are not promoted into primary model applicability. This is a dated snapshot, not automatic monitoring.
