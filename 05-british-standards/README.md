## How to use this register

- This is a checked register, not a complete catalogue of every British Standard or every historical amendment.
- Edition headings and publisher statuses are verified against public BSI records. Detailed changes are summarized only where the publisher supplies a public summary.
- An A-number in a designation records that amendment; it is not a complete list of earlier amendments or separate corrigenda.
- A publisher record date can refer to a corrected issue or product record and can differ from the year in the designation. It is not an effective or withdrawal date.
- Under review does not mean withdrawn. Drafts do not replace published standards. A newer edition does not establish a withdrawal date for an earlier one.
- Full standard texts require the publisher licence. Offline search stores this metadata and these original short summaries, not licensed standards PDFs.

## Maintenance

Edit `data/standards.json`, retaining earlier records and their evidence. Verify the exact edition on its publisher page, record the check date, and distinguish publication, amendment and withdrawal dates. Do not promote a consultation draft to a published standard or infer withdrawal from age. Run `python3 scripts/build_index.py`, `python3 scripts/validate.py` and the tests. Rebuilding does not recheck sources.

The register is a dated snapshot, with no automatic monitoring. Full standards are obtained from the publisher under its licence; the manuals downloader does not download them.
